About Me

I'm a Technician Video Game Developer in my final year of studies at UADE (Universidad de la Empresa Argentina), where I'm working towards my Bachelor's degree.

My main focus is 2D art, with experience in texturing, UI/UX and technical art. I also work with Unity and Unreal Engine, developing gameplay systems using C# and C++, as well as Blueprints in Unreal Engine.

I'm interested in the intersection of art, programming, and design, and I enjoy exploring how these disciplines come together during game development. I'm also interested in product management and team coordination, particularly in collaborative, multidisciplinary projects.
